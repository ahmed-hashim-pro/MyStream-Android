FAQ
How do I play audio?
Open any surah from the Quran tab, then tap the Play button at the bottom of the screen. You can choose your preferred reciter from the Reciters screen.
Can I read offline?
Yes. Download the reciter's audio files from the Reciters screen and the Mushaf will remain fully accessible without an internet connection.
Still need help? Email us.
